## File requirements

- **All vendors:** supply a vector PDF (or AI or EPS) at 100% size with a separate **cut line**. Art should run past the cut line (bleed) on every side, with text kept inside a safe zone. Vendors set their own bleed and safe-zone sizes, and I didn't check them, so look them up before exporting. Measure a real device and make an SVG template first, with the pin-pad cutout about 1 mm larger than the pad on every side, as `device-branding.md` says.
- **StickerApp:** interior cut-outs need a **PDF with the cutline** plus a comment on the order ([FAQ](https://stickerapp.com/support/artwork-design/is-it-possible-to-have-cut-outs-within-my-stickers)).
- **Sticker Mule:** **one** internal cut is allowed and must be at least 1/4" ([FAQ](https://www.stickermule.com/support/faq/custom-stickers/do-you-allow-internal-cuts)). Check that the pogo-pin hole is at least 1/4" (6.35 mm) across.
- **Wrap shops (WrapPrinting):** the cut path must be a spot color named **CutContour**, a 100% magenta hairline on its own layer. They accept PDF, AI, EPS, PSD and TIFF.
- Use the shortlist's brand colors (#184c30, #5c2414, #c9a54a, #f6f1e6) in the PDF. CMYK conversion and proof approval are standard at every vendor above that offers a proof.

**Takeaways for the art:**
- At ~6 pt the URL meets or matches every stated minimum.
- Being reversed at 85% opacity is the real risk. Flatten the gold to a solid CMYK tint rather than a transparency.
- Consider bumping the cap height to about 1.8–2 mm (~7–8 pt).
- Thicken the 0.17 mm hairline to at least 0.35 mm (1 pt). Sticker Mule calls 1 pt the minimum, and reversed lines need more.

### Recommendation

**StickerApp Removable** is the best fit:
- Exact 58 x 86 mm size.
- Low-tack removable adhesive with soft-touch matte.
- A 2 mm radius is achievable with an uploaded cutline.
- Prices: 25 = $34, 50 = $50, 100 = $74, with free shipping and about 1–2 day production. That is the same price as its permanent vinyl and the lowest 25 and 50 prices among the exact-size options.

Other options:
- StickerGiant can do an exact 2 mm radius, but it is permanent only and costs the most.
- Sticker Mule can't go below a 2.54 mm radius and has no removable option.
- UPrinting matte vinyl is cheapest at 100 ($59.35), but it is permanent and slightly undersized (2.25 x 3.25 in). Its removable BOPP starts at 100 ($71.45).

Before ordering anywhere, thicken the hairline and consider ~7–8 pt for the reversed URL.
