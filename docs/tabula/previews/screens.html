1 · No file explorer: launch straight into reading

Proposed flow (replaces today's Home list, where every item opens the file browser):
  1. Wake from sleep: back on the exact page you were reading. Nothing in between.
  2. First wake of a new day, or power-on: Hodie (Today). One screen with the feast, and big choices: Today's Mass, the Hour now (picked by the clock), and Continue.
  3. Select on Today's Mass: opens the propers EPUB at the Introit. Back returns to Hodie. That's the whole tree.
  4. Other days, other Hours, Order of Mass sit one level down (Plura / More), as named lists, never folders or filenames.
  5. Settings has three rows: Text size, Wi-Fi, About. Everything else stays at parish defaults.

2 · Three treatments

A · Claustrum (cloister): round Romanesque arches, voussoirs, an arcade along the edges. B · Chorus (choir): pointed lancets, quatrefoils, tracery. C · Tabula altaris (altar card): a ruled frame, corner crosses, Chi-Rho, rubric capitals. Each row is one treatment across the main screens.

3 · Button hints: three Catholic treatments

The four buttons sit under the hints at x = 38 / 154 / 268 / 384, each 106 × 40 px (the X3 layout in BaseTheme::drawButtonHints). Each treatment is shown at 1 : 1 width as a strip, then on the Today screen. Reading pages show no hints in any treatment.

4 · Sleep: Tabula screensaver

Drawn from the back-sticker takes, so the device's back and its resting screen match. All three share the sticker's rounded double frame, plain Latin cross, tracked TABULA, inscriptional MISSALE · BREVIARIVM and the Acutis mark. Green and gold become black and white on e-ink, so the dark takes are inverted. Latin throughout: the date is in Roman numerals, and the eight-Hour arc comes from the backmuse screensaver. Tap a screen to see it at 1 : 1. A sleeping screen uses no power.
