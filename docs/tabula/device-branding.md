# Device branding: back sticker or wrap (research, Oct 26, 2026)

## What we know about the X3 body
- About 98 × 64 × 5 mm, 58 g (Notebookcheck / Tuttotech: 4.98 mm; Pocketink: 5.1 mm).
- **Charging is through pogo pins on the back**, using a magnetic cable. The back also has magnets that let it stick to a phone.
- Power and reset are on the top edge (MakerWorld case notes). There's a microSD slot. Its exact position isn't published.
- No published drawing gives the pogo-pin position. **Measure a real unit** (see "Template" below).

## Options, from simplest
| Option | Covers | Pros | Cons | Rough cost for 50 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Small die-cut back sticker** (~45 × 45 mm logo, or 55 × 30 mm logo + "Tabula") | Center/upper back, away from the pins | No template needed. Sticker Mule / StickerApp quality. Durable laminated vinyl | Doesn't cover the whole back | ~$30–75 (Sticker Mule 3"×3" 50-pack was $72 list, $29 promo) |
| **B. Full-back skin** (back only, edge-to-edge, pin cutout) | Whole back | Premium "this is a Tabula" look. Hides the Xteink branding | Needs an accurate cut template. Thin vinyl only | Print + cut yourself (Cricut/Silhouette) or via a local sign shop |
| **C. Wrap (back + edges)** | Back and sides | Most custom | Edges carry buttons and the microSD slot. Many cutouts, and it peels at 5 mm-thin edges. **Not recommended** | — |
| **D. Branded case** | Whole device | Protection, plus logo debossed or UV-printed. MakerWorld has X3 case designs with a rear charging opening | 3D printing 50 cases takes time. Adds bulk | Filament + print time |

**Recommendation:** use **B** (a back-only skin with a pin cutout) for the premium units. Fall back to **A** if the template work slips. Avoid wrapping the edges.

## Material
- Printable cast wrap vinyl with an overlaminate: 3M IJ180Cv3 + 8518 matte, or Avery MPI 1105 + DOL 1360. Thickness ~0.1–0.15 mm. That won't noticeably weaken the magnets or lift the pogo cable.
- **The pogo cutout should be ~1 mm larger than the pin pad on every side.** The magnetic plug must seat flush. Stray vinyl edges near the pins are the main way a skin breaks charging.
- Use matte laminate: less glare on a device that lives in a pew.

## Artwork (Acutis Ignatius, new mark)
- Source: `~/Desktop/acutisIgnatiusv2.png` (1254 px, raster with a paper texture). **For print, trace a clean vector** (logo mark + wordmark, flat colors). Drop the paper texture. The gradient wordmark is fine on digital-print vinyl.
- Palette from the mark: forest green, leaf green, gold rays, with a red-brown end to the wordmark gradient.
- Suggested back layout (portrait): AI mark centered in the upper third, "Tabula" below in the serif, small "Acutis Ignatius · for [Parish name]" near the bottom. Keep 3 mm away from the edges and the pin cutout.
- Ask the pastor whether the parish name or crest should appear too. It's sold through the parish store.

## Template: how to get exact geometry
1. Put an X3 face-down on a flatbed scanner at 600 dpi with a ruler in the frame. Or photograph it straight down on a cutting mat.
2. Claude traces the outline (corner radius) and the pogo-pin pad into an SVG cut file at 1:1 mm, adding bleed and the 1 mm pin clearance.
3. Test-cut on plain vinyl. Check that the magnetic cable seats and charges. Adjust, then order the printed run.
